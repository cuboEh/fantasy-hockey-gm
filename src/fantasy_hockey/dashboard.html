How to read this board

Fantasy points use your league’s weights. ADP is the supplied Yahoo average draft position, not a promise that a player will be available. Projections may already account for injuries. Historical estimates and role scenarios are comparisons, not extra adjustments.

Check positional needs and workload, especially the three-appearance weekly goalie minimum. Season totals do not account for all games lost to full lineup nights.
